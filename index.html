Privacy Policy / Política de privacidad

Subidas El Nivel Oculto is a personal tool used only by the creator of El Nivel Oculto (YouTube @soyelniveloculto, Instagram @elniveloculto, TikTok @elniveloculto) to upload and schedule the creator's own videos on the creator's own accounts. It is not offered to other users.

  • Data used: only the permissions needed to publish videos on those accounts (YouTube upload, Instagram content publishing, TikTok video upload/publish) and basic profile data (name, avatar, username) to confirm the correct account is connected.
  • Storage: access tokens are stored only on the creator's own computer. No user data is collected, sold or shared with third parties.
  • Revocation: access can be removed at any time from the security settings of Google, Instagram/Meta or TikTok.

Es una herramienta personal: solo sube y programa los vídeos del propio creador en sus propias cuentas. Los tokens se guardan únicamente en su ordenador y no se comparten datos con terceros.

Terms of Service / Condiciones de uso

The tool is used exclusively to publish the creator's own content, in compliance with the terms of service of YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. Each post is created with the settings chosen by the creator (caption, privacy, interactions and content disclosure).

La herramienta se usa solo para publicar contenido propio, cumpliendo las condiciones de YouTube, Instagram y TikTok.
